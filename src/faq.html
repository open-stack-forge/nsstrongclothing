Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

Do you have a store I can visit?

No. NS Strong Clothing Company is online only. That keeps our costs and prices down. Most orders ship from Nova Scotia. A few items are printed and shipped from the US, and their product pages say so.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere in Canada. See Shipping & Returns for times and rates.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is $12.00 CAD within Canada. If your cart contains only sticker sheets, shipping is $8.99 CAD. Orders over $100 CAD (before taxes) ship free. Rates are shown in your cart and confirmed at checkout, with HST or GST added based on the shipping address. More detail is on our Shipping & Returns page.

How do your clothes fit?

Our tees and hoodies have a relaxed athletic fit and run true to size. If you're between sizes or like a looser fit, size up. Each product page lists fit details.

What currency are your prices in?

All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). Applicable sales taxes (GST/HST) and shipping are calculated at checkout.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by a secure third-party payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible. If your order hasn't shipped yet, we'll do our best to change or cancel it.

What is your return policy?

Unworn, unwashed items with tags can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Details are on our Shipping & Returns page.

Do you do team, club or bulk orders?

Yes, we'd love to. Send us a message with the quantity and timeline you have in mind.

Is my cart saved?

Yes. Your cart is saved in your browser on this device until you check out or clear it.
