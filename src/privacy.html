Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

NS Strong Clothing Company ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through nsstrongclothing.ca, how we use it, and your choices. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number (if given) and what you bought. Payment card details are entered directly with our payment processor and are never stored by us.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Cart data: your cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage) so it stays between visits. It isn't sent to us until you check out.
  • Technical data: our website host and network provider may automatically log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.

We don't use advertising cookies or sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, including returns and refunds.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • With your consent only, to send you news and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We share only what's needed with service providers who help us run the store:

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Printful produces and ships your order. For delivery, we provide your name, shipping address, email and phone number as needed. Printful may process this information in Canada, the United States and other countries.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our business email.
  • Cloudflare provides website and checkout infrastructure.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Resend may send transactional email, such as order and shipping updates.

Some of these providers may store or process personal information outside Canada, including in the United States and other countries, where it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions. We may also disclose information when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep order records and accounting records for six years to meet Canadian tax requirements, and other personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate or incomplete. You may also withdraw consent to marketing at any time. To make a request, contact our Privacy Officer at the email below.

Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, please contact our Privacy Officer first so we can try to resolve it. You may also file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

Our site uses HTTPS encryption, and payments are processed by a PCI-compliant provider. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

Privacy Officer

Our Privacy Officer is Calvin Gould. Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to info@nsstrongclothing.ca. We do not publish a street address for privacy enquiries; please contact us by email.
