Our story

Strength, community and pride in the place we call home.

NS Strong Clothing Company was started in Nova Scotia with a simple idea: make honest, hard-wearing apparel that celebrates the people of this province. The fishers and nurses, the hockey parents and early-morning lifters, the folks who shovel out their neighbours after a nor'easter.

Nova Scotia strong isn't just a slogan. It's how people here get through long winters, rough seas and hard seasons together. Our gear is built for that kind of life: comfortable enough for every day and tough enough for the days that test you.

How we work

  • Online only. We don't have a storefront, which keeps our costs down and our prices fair.
  • Shipped from Nova Scotia. Orders are packed here and sent across Canada.
  • Small batches. We release designs in limited runs, so when something's gone, it may be gone for good.
