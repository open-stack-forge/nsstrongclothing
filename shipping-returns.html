Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about getting your gear, and sending it back if it isn't right.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses within Canada. Items are designed in Nova Scotia and printed in Canada. A few items, marked on their product page, are printed and shipped from the United States.

Items shipped from the US. These items arrive in a separate parcel from the rest of your order, typically within about two weeks. Because they cross the border, Canadian duties, GST/HST or carrier brokerage fees may be charged on delivery by the carrier or customs. Those charges are not included in your order total at checkout and are not collected by us.

Processing time

Orders are usually packed and shipped within 2–5 business days. Limited-run and pre-order items may take longer; any extra time will be noted on the product page.

Shipping rates and delivery

  • Standard shipping within Canada is $12.00 CAD.
  • Sticker-only orders (carts that contain only sticker sheets) ship for $8.99 CAD.
  • Free standard shipping within Canada on orders over $100 CAD, including sticker-only orders.
  • Shipping is shown in your cart and confirmed at checkout before you pay.
  • Typical delivery after shipping: 2–4 business days in Atlantic Canada and 3–8 business days elsewhere in Canada. Remote areas may take longer.
  • You'll get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Returns and exchanges

  • You may return unworn, unwashed items with original tags within 30 days of delivery.
  • To start a return or exchange, contact us with your order number. We'll send return instructions.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after we receive and inspect the return.
  • Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged, defective or wasn't what you ordered.
  • Final-sale items (marked on the product page) and gift cards can't be returned.

Damaged or wrong items

If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we'll make it right at no cost to you.

Lost packages

If your tracking hasn't updated in 7 business days, contact us and we'll work with the carrier to find it or send a replacement.
